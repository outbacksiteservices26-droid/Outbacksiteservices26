Post-construction cleaning across Utah

A cleaner handoff for Utah construction projects.

Outback Site Services helps builders, general contractors, property managers, and homeowners prepare new and renovated spaces for inspection, turnover, and move-in. We review residential and commercial projects statewide, including Tooele County, Salt Lake City, Park City, Heber City, Logan, St. George, and surrounding Utah communities.

What does a final construction clean include?

Final cleans can include construction-dust removal, floors, fixtures, cabinets, trim, interior windows, tracks, kitchens, bathrooms, and detailed finish work based on the project scope.

Do you quote projects throughout Utah?

Yes. Availability and travel are based on the project location, size, schedule, and required services. Share the city and scope through the quote builder for a project-specific response.

Can services be combined?

Yes. Post-construction cleaning can be paired with exterior job-site cleanup, pressure washing, carpet shampooing, window cleaning, appliance interiors, and other requested add-ons.
